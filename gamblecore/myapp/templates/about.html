About 🎰

Gamblecore is a competitive and pixelated gambling game. It features many games, an account system to save your progress history and your bests, long-term and short-term

leaderboards, two main versions depending on what day it is, and much more. Our mission is to give and learn people the fun of gambling, by creating our own gambling games that

addresses most of the idea of them.


Our Story 📖

It all started with a single group of Middle School students–well, make that 4 students: Alvin, Jacob, Justin, and Matías.


After an incredibly boring week at Middle School, students were desperate about unblocked gambling games and being competitive in something non-athletic. A student in Middle

School had a brilliant idea: What if we, the students, make our gambling games and only students can have access to it?


It could be challenging, but the students were up to it.


Everyone loved Alvin's brilliant idea, but not everyone helped to contribute to make that happen. Justin was up for Alvin's brilliant idea, he decided he would include gambling games in

his Google Document, Project Existence. Project Existence was great for Alvin's brilliant idea, because Project Existence already had most of the students that ONLY students can

have access to.


Half of Alvin's brilliant idea was completed, but the students needed to figure out how to make the gambling games.


Then, a student decided to help make the gambling games and contribute through the development of Alvin's brilliant idea. That student's name was Matías.


During school the next day, Justin had told Matías about Alvin's idea.

Justin suggested, “A lottery could be great”. But Matías thought to himself that was too simple.

“Hmm… I don't know. What about a slot machine?” Matías asked Justin.

Justin took a moment, and then answered, “Sure, that could work. As long as it is not too hard.”


After school the same day, Matías called Justin for a Google Meet. With Matias' programming skills in Python and Justin's IQ in video games and online resources, they decided to

work together. After a loooooooooooong time figuring out how to properly have a simple slot machine logic and a good-working online compiler for Python, Justin and Matḯas finally

had something: a slot machine.


But there was one concern from Justin: The symbols are too boring and uncolorful.

“Well, that's the best I can do. I don't know how to have colorful symbols in Python.” Matías told Justin.

“What do you mean? Emojis do exist, since 1999, and you're saying that you don't know how to have colorful symbols?” Justin exclaimed.

“Fine. If you really want to, you can put Emojis instead. You'll see you'll get a syntax error”, Matías explained, confidently to Justin.
