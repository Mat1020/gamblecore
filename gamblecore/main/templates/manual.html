📒 Gambling Arcades Informational Notes


🎰 Slot Machines: 🎰

Name: Difficulty to Learn: Difficulty to Win: Wins: Description:
Classic Slot Machine Easy Easy 1 With 3 columns, and 8 different symbols, Classic Slot Machine is considered the most practical of both Slot Machines and Gambling Arcades.
Bucks Slot Machine Hard Medium 3 Different from the other 2, Bucks Slot Machine is known for having 3 rows and 3 columns, which you can bet on different rows (lines) up to a amount of $100.
Tryhard Slot Machine Easy Hard 7 Exactly like Classic Slot Machine, but Tryhard Slot Machine adds 2 more columns (5 columns total) for decreasing winning chances, however it has 6 symbols instead of 8 symbols.

🎡 Roulette Wheels: 🎡

Name: Difficulty to Learn: Difficulty to Win: Wins: Description:
French Roulette Wheel Easy/Medium Easy 3 With a random chance of hitting 0 for its uniqueness, French Roulette Wheel stands out as giving better odds to the player but is the most complex to strategice. The winning chances are: 1.35% (with La Partage/En Prison) or 2.70% (most of the time).
American Roulette Wheel Easy Medium 7 Exactly like European Roulette Wheel, but American Roulette Wheel decreases winning chances by adding the opportunity to guess double zero (00). The winning chances are: 2.63%.
European Roulette Wheel Easy Easy 1 May be the father of Roulette Wheels, European Roulette Wheel is the simplest to learn about Roulette Wheels and the easiest to win. The winning chances are: 2.70%

🃏 Poker Variants: 🃏

Name: Difficulty to Learn: Difficulty to Win: Wins: Description:
Texas Hold'em Easy/Medium Easy 2 With 2-cards, an interesting betting & calling system, and forced bets (Blinds), Texas Hold'em is considered the Top #1 in popularity, being the most famous Poker Variant.
Omaha Hi Easy Easy 1 A twin of Texas Hold'em, Omaha Hi doesn't have Blinds for easier understanding of Poker and has 4-cards. Omaha Hi is considered Top #2 in popularity.
Seven-Card Stud Easy Hard 7 Known for having 7-cards, Seven-Card Stud has 5 small-rounds in 1 round, bets count up, and Antes' amount are random (Antes are the same as Blinds). Seven-Card Stud is considered Top #3 in popularity.
Five-Card Draw *Cantredraw Medium Medium 3 More commonly known as Cantredraw, Cantredraw has 5-cards and has everything mentioned earlier. It has a Drawing system in which goes like this: you can draw some of your cards and receive new cards from the deck to improve your hand. Cantredraw is considered Top #4 in popularity.

👀 Gamblecore Features Explained


Do you want to know cool features about Gamblecore? Or maybe you don't know some features and you need to know them? Well, that is what this section is going to be mostly

about. The main idea of this section of the Manual is to explain every important or interesting feature Gamblecore has. Below, you can see how these features are identified:

Interesting Features Important Features
Single asterisk (*) Doble asterisk (**)

Gambling Categories **

Gambling Categories is the name we have for our general playing sections. In total, we have 3 Gambling Categories; Slot Machines, Roulette Wheels, and Poker Variants. The

Gambling Categories are a base line for our Leaderboards' organization.


Gambling Arcades **

Gambling Arcades is the name we have for our games for each Gambling Category, we recomend you read Gambling Categories ** first. We have a total of 10 Gambling Arcades;

Classic Slot Machine, Bucks Slot Machine, Tryhard Slot Machine (3 for Slot Machines), French Roulette Wheel, American Roulette Wheel, European Roulette Whell (3 for Roulette

Wheels), Texas Hold'em, Omaha Hi, Seven-Card Stud, and Cantredraw (4 for Poker Variants).


Stats **

The Stats is what you see after you quit playing, tells you and shows you what you did, later send that information to Gamblecore to record you in the Leaderboards. The Stats are

surrounded by asterisks and includes the initials of the Gambling Arcade you played (example if you chose Classic Slot Machine: * CSM Stats* ). After that, the Stats says how much

money you left with, how many wins, how many loses, how many ties or misses, and the total rounds. After it tells and shows you an overall summary about your run, the Stats tells

you a warmly good-bye.


Warning *

The Warning's general purpose is to make sure that people understand that there's no betting real money and there are no prizes for legal reasons, the message overall is very

straightfoward. If you have any problems reading it, here's the Warning's message: *Warning: This isn't Gambling as there is no betting real money; The following contents may be

addictive, play responsibly & we are not liable for any damages. Please don't gamble in real life or bet real money, it causes a lot of harm. There are no prizes for legal reasons, and

the experience is intended solely for entertainment & competitive purposes* Additionally, the Warning's design represents a yellow note on the side or front of an slot machine or

arcades that include real gambling.


Leaderboards **

The way that the Leaderboards work in Gamblecore is quite complex, so we'll try our best to explain it to you; there are two types of Leaderboards: Complete Leaderboards (shows

every player who was recorded) and Top-5 Leaderboards (shows players who are in top 5, self explanatory). The Top-5 leaderboards use Complete Leaderboards as its guide. On the

other hand, Complete Leaderboards do the requirements for a player to be recorded and gets the information from the Stats. Now that's out of the way, let's talk how to "read" Top-5

Leaderboards. The first thing we have, is the place number, and the player name, seperated by a space and a column. After the place number and player name, we have the amount

of wins, seperated by parenthesis. The max wins for a player in each Gambling Category each day is 7 wins.


Weekend Leaderboards *

The Weekend Leaderboards don't work too diferent from the regular Leaderboards, in fact, there are small changes. The main idea of the Weekend Leaderboards is to combine all the

Gambling Categories' wins together (Slot Machines wins + Roulette Wheels wins + Poker Variants wins = your total wins). The other main idea is that the max wins is 27 instead of 7

wins. Additionally, instead of having Top-5 Leaderboards, Weekend has Top-10 Leaderboards. The changes are the same for the Complete Leaderboards for the weekend.


❓ Common Problems


Q: Why does the total rounds don't make sense? Why does it not match my wins, losses, misses/ties (overall Stats)?


A: The total number of rounds may look confusing to you for two main reasons. The first reason is the win multiplier;

you can win once, but it counts as more. The second reason is that you didn't input a valid action, which in some

(not all) Gambling Arcades can lead to just starting a new round.

🗺️ Where Should YOU Start?

Image gotten from: SmartDraw

Overall:

If you don't know where to start, we suggest that everyone new to Gamblecore try the Classic Slot Machine, as it's one of the simplest Gambling Arcades. Another option similar to the

Classic Slot Machine is the Tryhard Slot Machine, which is more challenging in terms of winning time. Before exploring other Gambling Categories, you should try the Bucks Slot

Machine at least once, as it's a fun way to learn how to bet.


Next, you might consider trying the European Roulette Wheel or Omaha Hi, as both are among the simplest and easiest options in their respective Gambling Categories. If you enjoy

the European Roulette Wheel, you can then explore the American Roulette Wheel or the French Roulette Wheel. If you prefer Omaha Hi, you can move on to Texas Hold'em, and

later try Cantredraw or Seven-Card Stud.


Remember, there are 10 different and unique Gambling Arcades. It really depends on your personal style, but the important part is to try each one and enjoy playing them! :D
